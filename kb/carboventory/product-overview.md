# Carboventory – turn reuse into carbon proof

**One line:** Carboventory helps ESG and facilities teams track reused assets (furniture, electronics, fixtures, equipment) and quantify the carbon saved versus buying new, using EPD baselines, Caly AI and lifecycle reports. Part of the Carbalyze family (launched March 2026).

## Who it is for
- ESG, sustainability, facilities and workplace teams that already reuse furniture and assets.
- Furniture dealers, manufacturers' circular / take-back programmes and dealer networks.

## The problem ("the circular economy gap")
- Organisations reuse assets but **cannot prove or quantify** the carbon saved.
- Asset records are scattered across spreadsheets, email and facilities systems.
- ESG reporting asks for circularity data; teams compile it manually.

## Four pillars
1. **Asset inventory** – one place for every reused asset: location, condition, composition, lifecycle history. Bulk import from CSV/Excel.
2. **Carbon & EPD measurement** – CO₂e saved per reuse event using Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) baselines vs buying new.
3. **Caly AI insights** – ask in plain language ("What's my carbon saved this quarter?").
4. **ESG lifecycle reporting** – reuse rates and carbon impact, PDF and Excel export.

## How the carbon number is built (from blogs)
EPD match for the asset → industry baseline when no exact EPD → AI estimate as last resort, each with a confidence level and audit trail. EPD Lookup searches EPD databases by category (furniture, electronics, fixtures, lighting).

## Safe messaging
- "Every asset reused is carbon saved – but only if you can measure it."
- Lead with proof and traceability (EPD-backed, confidence-scored), not big numbers.

## Do NOT use
- The demo dashboard numbers on the homepage (48.2t CO₂e, 1,247 assets, 87% reuse, "↑12% MoM", "workstations saved 18.4t") – these are **sample data**, not customer results.
- "Compliance verified", "compliance-ready" as a guarantee.
- Customer names or volumes (e.g. One Workplace) unless the user supplies an approved statement.
