# PCF cost factors – summary

**Core idea:** Two teams can both "do a PCF" with very different effort. Cost = money (consultants, software, verification) + time + people across teams + upkeep when products change.

## The five factors
1. **Scope and system boundary** – broader boundaries (cradle-to-grave, use phase, end of life) add data and assumptions. Fix: pick the boundary from the decision the PCF must support; document it before starting.
2. **Data quality (primary vs secondary)** – primary data needs coordination across teams and suppliers; secondary data still needs checking. Fix: prioritise inputs that move the result, use documented secondary data where permitted, improve over time.
3. **Product and portfolio complexity** – deep multi-level BOMs, many suppliers, variants, hundreds of SKUs. Fix: standard BOM formats and material names, variant rules, repeatable workflows.
4. **Methodology, documentation, verification** – allocation, audit trail, external review add work. Fix: define method and documentation up front; keep the audit trail as you go.
5. **Calculation approach** – spreadsheets, consultants, software or a mix. Software helps repetition at scale but doesn't remove data, judgement or review.

## Mistakes that raise cost
No boundary up front · collecting every possible data point · inconsistent BOMs · documentation left to the end · treating every variant separately · ignoring future updates.
