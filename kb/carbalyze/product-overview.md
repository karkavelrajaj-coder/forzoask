# Carbalyze – Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) with Caly

**One line:** Carbalyze turns a Bill of Materials (BOM) into a product carbon footprint (Scope 1–3) with reduction ideas, using Caly, an AI sustainability assistant. Built by Forzo Techlabs.

## Who it is for
- Manufacturers and suppliers, especially SMEs, in the US and Europe (Germany, France, UK pages exist).
- Sustainability, operations and product teams who are not LCA experts.
- Teams answering customer PCF requests or building Scope 3 data.

## The problem it addresses
- Supplier data arrives in inconsistent spreadsheets with missing fields.
- Traditional life cycle assessments take weeks of manual data collection.
- Scope 3 data is hard to verify and there is no central tracking.
- Matching each BOM material to an emission factor by hand is a bottleneck.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Upload the BOM** (Excel or CSV) – raw materials, components or finished products.
2. **Caly maps materials to emission factors** using industry emission-factor databases (the FAQ mentions a database of 10,000+ emission factor values – confirm before using the number publicly) and calculates Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, highlighting hotspots.
3. **Get a report and recommendations** – exportable report with emission hotspots and ideas to reduce emissions.

## Features listed on the site
- Product Carbon Footprint calculation, including multi-level BOMs
- Supplier PCF data request workflow; use supplier-specific data or global emission factors
- Caly AI assistant (chat), smart recommendations on emission hotspots
- Automated carbon reports (CSV, PDF) and data exports
- Emission factor database integration
- Role-based access control
- **CBAM Invoice Analyzer:** upload an invoice (or use a sample) to standardise emissions data for EU CBAM reporting
- PLM integration via a built-in MCP server is described in a Sept 2025 blog (confirm current status before promoting)

- Methods **aligned with** the GHG Protocol (Product Standard, Scope 1–3) and ISO 14067.
