# Carbalyze plans – context only

Do not put prices in marketing posts. Send people to a demo.

- Team-based access, **no per-seat fees** (safe to mention).
- **Launch** plan: for getting started. Includes PCF calculation, multi-level BOM PCF, supplier PCF data requests, BOM and emission factor upload, Caly assistant, hotspot recommendations, CSV/PDF reports, data exports, Scope 1–2 disclosure support, emission factor database integration, basic branding, role-based access.
- **Ascend** plan: everything in Launch plus predictive analysis for PCF improvement, advanced Scope 3 reporting (purchased goods), ERP/PLM integration, advanced branding, dedicated customer success manager, priority support, SSO.
- Every plan includes Caly.
