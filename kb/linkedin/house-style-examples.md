# LinkedIn house style

Our recent Carbalyze page posts are accurate and well scoped. Keep their strengths and fix their weaknesses.

## Keep (strengths)
- Hook names a concrete situation: "Same plant data. Different carbon results per product, depending on the allocation method."
- Careful, honest wording: "Submission is not approval." "These are NAW's arguments, not court findings."
- Methods and sources named (ISO 14044, GHG Protocol Product Standard, CAA, CalRecycle).
- Numbered points (1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣) with one short explanation each.
- Practical takeaway line ("Document your chosen allocation method and why it fits the process.").

## Fix (weaknesses to avoid)
- Generic closing questions ("What are you looking forward to most…?") get little engagement. Ask a specific, answerable question or skip it.
- No product link: posts end with a blog link or a question but never show how Carbalyze / Packaging Intelligence helps. Add ONE soft product line + CTA.
- Too many hashtags (10–12). Use 3–8.
- Blog promotion posts that repeat the whole blog. Give one strong insight and send readers to the blog for the rest.
- Weekly roundups that mix four unrelated topics dilute the message. Prefer one topic per post.

## Example structure that works
```
<Hook: a real buyer problem in one line>

<2–3 lines of context: why it matters now>

<3 numbered points or a short how-to>

<One takeaway line>

<One soft product line + CTA>

<3–8 hashtags>
```
