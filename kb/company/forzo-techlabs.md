# Forzo Techlabs (FTL)

**One line:** Forzo Techlabs helps US and European businesses use AI agents and agentic workflows to automate operations, and builds its own carbon products under the Carbalyze brand.

## Basics
- Tagline: "Experts in Digital Solutioning".
- US address: 41430 Grand River Ave, Ste D, Novi, MI 48375. Team works from India (remote).
- Contact: info@forzotechlabs.com · LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/forzotechlabs/
- Two lines of business: (1) custom software and AI services for clients; (2) own products – Carbalyze (PCF), Carboventory (asset reuse), Packaging Intelligence (EPR).

## Services described on the site
- Agentic workflow design: mapping business workflows and automating them with AI agents.
- AI agent / multi-agent development, including agents that work with legacy APIs, databases and ERP systems.
- Generative UI (interfaces that adapt to the user's request).
- AI guardrails and testing against hallucination and drift.
- Human-in-the-loop design: agents verify answers against company data and ask for human approval on high-stakes actions.
