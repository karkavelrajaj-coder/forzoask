# Packaging Intelligence (EPR) – product overview

**One line:** Packaging EPR software that turns a packaging Bill of Materials into Circular Action Alliance (CAA) supply data: it classifies packaging, flags producer obligation in Oregon, Colorado and California, estimates base program fees and exports a worksheet. You still file on the CAA producer portal.

**House-style note:** this site's honest, specific tone is the model for all our content.

## Who it is for
Brand owners, manufacturers and distributors that put covered packaging and paper into Oregon, Colorado or California.

## The problem it solves
1. **BOMs are not portal reports.** Engineering files list SKUs and components; CAA needs net weight by material category into each state.
2. **Obligation is unclear until you classify.** Companies need Obligated / Exempt / Unknown per state before registering.
3. **Invoices arrive late.** Finance needs a base-fee estimate earlier than the official invoice.

## How it works
1. **Upload the packaging BOM** – CSV, Excel or a short manual table (SKU, component, material, weight). Unusual headers are mapped first.
2. **Classify materials and obligation** – free-text materials normalised to CAA categories; Obligated / Exempt / Unknown for OR, CO, CA. Unrecognised resins go to a review picklist – never silently assigned.
3. **Estimate fees and export** – annual pounds × published CAA base rate per category; PDF, Excel and CSV export for portal entry.

Worked example from the site: a snack SKU shipping 250,000 units with a 0.06 lb carton and a 0.012 lb film pouch = 15,000 lb corrugated fiber + 3,000 lb flexible plastic, then split by destination state.

## What the user brings
SKU · component · material · weight + unit (g, kg, oz, lb) · annual units into each state (often confirmed after upload), plus revenue and producer role.

## What it does NOT do (say this clearly – it builds trust)
- Not the filing portal: no auto-submit; registration, reporting and payment stay with CAA.
- Not legal advice: counsel or CAA confirms obligation.
- Not an official invoice: base-rate estimates only. **Eco-modulation, PCR discounts, reuse credits and bonuses are excluded** in this release so the figure is not understated.

## Useful facts from the site
- Oregon SB 582 covers packaging and paper across about 60 material categories, with roughly a two-year lag between supply year and billing.
- Estimates are labelled by data year.
- CAA reporting has four tracks: legal agreements, supply data reports (our core output), eco-modulation evidence, vendor and billing.
